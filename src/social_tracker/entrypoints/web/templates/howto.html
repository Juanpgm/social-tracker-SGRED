How to register a source

Pick the type that matches where the content comes from. Nothing is collected until you enable the source, and nothing is ever published or sent anywhere.

News RSS feed

What to enter

The address of an RSS or Atom feed (http or https) and a name. Optionally a list of keywords, one per line, to focus on.

Example

https://example.org/rss.xml

Limits

The feed is checked at most every {{ news_poll_min }} seconds (default {{ news_poll_default }}). Addresses that embed a login or a secret in the query string are refused, and so are file: addresses.

Instagram account

What to enter

The account handle (letters, digits, dots, underscores) and a secret reference, which names where the login is kept: env:NAME (an environment variable), keyring:NAME or file:NAME. Never type the password into this form.

Example

Handle some.handle, reference env:IG_MAIN.

Limits

Collection from Instagram is best-effort: it can stop working without notice and is checked at most every {{ instagram_poll_min }} seconds (default {{ instagram_poll_default }}). Use a dedicated account, not a personal one. Posts and stories only: no Lives. The credentials stay on this machine, in the place the reference points to, and are never tracked by git or shown in this interface.

Live audio stream

What to enter

The address of a direct audio stream (http, https, rtmp or icecast), such as the Icecast mount point of a radio station. A web page that plays the stream is not enough: the address must serve the audio itself. Playlists such as HLS (.m3u8) are not a direct stream.

Example

https://stream.example.org/radio.mp3

Limits

Audio is cut into chunks of {{ chunk_min }} to {{ chunk_max }} seconds (default {{ chunk_default }}). The language is a tag such as es (default {{ language_default }}). Video-only or login-protected streams do not work.

Recorded audio

What to enter

Either the address of a podcast-style feed or the path of a local directory that holds audio files. Give one of them, not both.

Example

https://example.org/podcast.xml or /srv/audio/incoming

Limits

A feed is checked at most every {{ recorded_poll_min }} second (default {{ recorded_poll_default }}). Files are processed in batches, not in real time. Common audio formats such as MP3, M4A, WAV, OGG and FLAC are expected; a file that cannot be decoded is skipped.
